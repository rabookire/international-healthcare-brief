## Source limitations

- Undated official pages retain a blank source date; last verified is the date checked, not the publication date.
- A baseline marker identifies a pre-existing arrangement; it does not establish that a planned facility is operational. Read each record’s source-scope note.
- Historical candidate events that cannot meet the sourcing standard remain excluded or on hold.
- Baseline records are visually muted and can be filtered independently from new activity.
- Backfill records retain original announcement dates. Updates to older deals carry an evidenced latest-event date and never create duplicate records or backdated editions.
