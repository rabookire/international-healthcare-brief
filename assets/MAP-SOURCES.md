# Map sources

Country boundaries: Natural Earth, 1:110m countries; public domain. Retrieved 2026-10-20 from https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson. Coordinates rounded to 0.01 degrees; Antarctica omitted. Terms: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/LICENSE.md.

The map uses an equirectangular projection, longitude −180° to +180°, with north at the top. Boundaries are for orientation and do not express a position on sovereignty. City points are approximate locators, not facility addresses. Where a city location is unavailable, a country locator is used and labeled. Nearby pins are displaced with leader lines; exact locator dots remain at the approximate city/country position. Country selection always has a complete list alternative, including small islands absent at this boundary scale.

All geometry and application assets are served locally. No tile service, tracking, geolocation, analytics, or third-party requests are required.


## Reader 2026-10-22.2

World/regional views use anchored screen-space clusters of approximate locators; counts represent unique relationships in each cluster. A relationship spanning several documented served markets can have multiple locators, so map labels must not be summed into a global total. Country-level coordinates added for coverage use the existing Natural Earth feature label coordinates. They do not establish a facility address. The equivalent activity list preserves every matching relationship.

Activity icons (home, briefcase, users, shield, globe, link and book-open) are vendored from [official Feather icons](https://github.com/feathericons/feather/tree/main/icons), under [the existing MIT license](icons/LICENSE.txt). They indicate activity categories, not hospital brands or endorsement.
